Our Approach

How we care for you

A structured, compassionate pathway from first contact to ongoing wellbeing.

1
GP Referral

Obtain a referral from your GP or specialist. Required for Medicare rebates and gives us your history before we meet.

2
Initial Assessment

A thorough psychiatric assessment — in clinic or via telehealth — to understand your concerns, history, and goals.

3
Treatment Plan

A personalised, collaborative plan developed with you, your family, and your referrer — clear and practical.

4
Ongoing Review

Regular follow-up to monitor progress, adjust treatment, and provide continued support across the lifespan.
